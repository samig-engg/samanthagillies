About Me

Hello! My name is Sam and I am a second-year mechanical engineer student at the University of Alberta. I am originally from Regina Saskatchewan which is where I worked this past summer as a mechanical designer trainee at PTI Transformers. When I am not studying for my next exam, I enjoy playing dodgeball or relaxing with a good fantasy novel. I have recently joined Alberta Heart, a project club that is developing a total artificial heart. In high school I was on the volleyball team and was the media coordinator for the football team. Outside of high school I did competitive dance for 15 years and played, as well as coached, roller derby. I became interested in mechanical engineering after taking an automotive shop class offered by my school when I was 15. Since then, I have taken three shop classes where I have learnt the basics of car maintenance and repairs. I hope to use this knowledge of systems to improve my perspective as an engineer.
